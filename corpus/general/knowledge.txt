Knowledge is information that has been learned through observation, experience, education, or investigation. People acquire knowledge by reading, listening, experimenting, asking questions, and solving problems.

Learning is a process through which information and skills become easier to understand and use. Effective learning usually requires practice, feedback, and repeated exposure to important ideas.

A fact is a statement that can be verified using reliable evidence. An opinion expresses a person's judgment or belief. Distinguishing facts from opinions is important when evaluating information.

Problem solving involves identifying a problem, understanding its causes, considering possible solutions, and selecting an appropriate approach. Good problem solving often requires logical reasoning and careful analysis.

Communication allows people and computer systems to exchange information. Written communication uses words and symbols, while computers can also communicate through structured data such as JSON and XML.

Curiosity encourages people to explore unfamiliar subjects. Asking clear questions is one of the most useful ways to improve understanding. When a question is difficult, breaking it into smaller questions can make it easier to solve.

Critical thinking involves examining evidence, identifying assumptions, comparing alternatives, and reaching conclusions based on available information. It is useful in science, engineering, programming, education, and everyday decision making.
