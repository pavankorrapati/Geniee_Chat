Maya: What are you working on today?

Arun: I am building a small language model.

Maya: What does the model learn?

Arun: It learns patterns in sequences of tokens and tries to predict the next token.

Maya: Does that mean it understands everything?

Arun: No. Predicting the next token is different from having reliable understanding.

Maya: Then how can it become a chatbot?

Arun: A base language model can later be trained with instruction and conversation data.

Maya: What are you testing first?

Arun: I am checking the tokenizer, dataset, model, training loop, and text generation separately.

Maya: That sounds useful.

Arun: Yes. Debugging each component separately makes it easier to understand where a problem comes from.

Maya: What happens if the generated text is irrelevant?

Arun: I would inspect the training data, tokenization, training loss, checkpoint, and generation settings before changing everything.

Maya: That is a good approach.

Arun: Building a language model is easier when each stage is measured and tested instead of being treated as one large system.
